Emulator

This ROM is only published in the release archive of kiwisincebirth/TRS-80-ROMS, which your browser cannot download directly. 1. Download TRS-80-…-ROM-Images.tar from the latest release. 2. Open the archive here; will be extracted from it:

ROMs in the list are downloaded from kiwisincebirth/TRS-80-ROMS when you choose them. They are © Tandy / Microsoft.

Load the Level II ROM first.

Drive 0 is the boot drive: inserting a disk there restarts the TRS-80 on it. JV1, JV3 and DMK images, single or double density. As on a real Model I, track 0 must be single density to boot.

    Disks, programs (.CMD, .CAS) and BASIC listings (.BAS) you keep are stored in this browser, on this device only. Nothing is uploaded.

      The repository has the folders rom/, disk/, cmd/ and bas/, each with an index.json listing its files. Files from your device: use the Load… and Insert… buttons, or My library.
