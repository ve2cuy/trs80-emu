Programs

Load the Level II ROM first.

Disks

Drive 0 is the boot drive: inserting a disk there restarts the TRS-80 on it. Disk images: JV1, JV3 and DMK, whatever their extension (.DSK, .DMK…), single or double density (Percom and Radio Shack doublers). As on a real Model I, track 0 must be single density to boot.

Keyboard
TRS-80PC
ENTEREnter
BREAKEsc or End
CLEARHome or Delete
← (backspace)Backspace or Left arrow
→ (tab)Tab or Right arrow
↑ ↓Up and Down arrows

Type symbols as you would on a PC: the emulator handles the TRS-80 Shift key, whose layout is different.

On a tablet or phone, tap the screen (or “⌨ Keyboard”) to show the on-screen keyboard; the buttons under the screen give BREAK, CLEAR, the arrows and ENTER.
